## 5. What these sources do NOT say (important limitations)

1. **No approval probability.** Google publishes no formula, threshold or percentage that converts site characteristics into an approval probability. Any "X% chance of AdSense approval" figure is invented.
2. **No minimum word count, no minimum page count, no minimum traffic figure** is published as an AdSense application requirement. Google asks for high-quality, original, useful, unique content with clear navigation.
3. **No official "site quality score".** The 0–100 AdSense Readiness Score used in `docs/ADSENSE-FINAL-AUDIT.md` is this audit's internal score, not a Google score.
4. **Google does not pre-approve content.** Application review happens after submission, and Google may change policies at any time (explicitly stated in the eligibility and program-policy pages).
5. **Traffic quality and applicant identity/age cannot be verified from the repository.** They are marked as owner responsibilities.
6. Search result snippets from `support.google.com` were used only to confirm page titles and canonical URLs; substantive policy text was read from the Google-owned pages themselves.
